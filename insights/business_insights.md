# Olist SQL Mini Capstone – Business Insights

## Data Overview

The Olist Brazilian E-commerce dataset contains transactional data from an online marketplace, including information about customers, orders, payments, products, and order items. This analysis used multiple related tables joined through primary and foreign keys to answer business questions about customer behavior and sales performance. The dataset covers purchases made over multiple months, allowing revenue trend and customer purchasing analyses over time. Data was analyzed using MySQL using joins, aggregations, common table expressions (CTEs), window functions, and conditional logic.

---

## Business Insight 1: A Small Group of Customers Generates the Highest Revenue

The analysis of the top 10 customers by total spending shows that customer spending is not evenly distributed. A relatively small number of customers contribute significantly more revenue than the average customer. This suggests that high-value customers play an important role in overall sales performance and should be considered a priority for customer retention initiatives.

---

## Business Insight 2: Revenue Changes Across Different Months

Monthly revenue analysis shows that sales fluctuate over time instead of remaining consistent each month. Some months generate noticeably higher revenue than others, indicating possible seasonal demand or the impact of promotions and marketing campaigns. Monitoring monthly trends helps businesses identify peak sales periods and plan inventory and marketing activities more effectively.

---

## Business Insight 3: Revenue is Concentrated in High-Performing Product Categories

Revenue analysis by product category shows that some categories contribute a much larger share of total revenue than others. These categories are the primary revenue drivers for the business and have a greater impact on overall sales performance. Understanding category performance helps prioritize inventory management and promotional efforts for products with the greatest business value.

---

## Recommendation 1: Strengthen Customer Retention Strategies

Develop loyalty programs, personalized discounts, or reward campaigns for customers with higher spending and repeat purchases. Retaining existing high-value customers can increase long-term revenue and improve customer lifetime value.

---

## Recommendation 2: Focus Marketing on High-Revenue Categories and Peak Sales Months

Increase marketing efforts and inventory planning for product categories that generate the highest revenue, especially during months with historically strong sales performance. Using historical revenue trends can help optimize promotions and product availability during peak demand periods.
